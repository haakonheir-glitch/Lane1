# Vision

**Lane's purpose.** Human biology evolved for work you could see, understand and feel: varied, whole, meaningful, self-directed, with immediate feedback. Modern work has tens of thousands of job titles, abstract output and almost no feedback, and people choose their path with little information about themselves. Lane measures how a person is wired (interests, personality, work values and reasoning, using validated instruments) and answers three questions:

1. **How am I wired?** The profile report.
2. **How do I work best?** The kind of value I'm built to create, and what my best workday looks like.
3. **Where does that work exist?** The career paths that best match my results.

The science behind it is person–environment fit, which has two parts:

- **needs–supplies fit:** does the work give me what I need? This predicts satisfaction and fulfilment, and it's the headline.
- **demands–abilities fit:** can I meet what the work demands? This is the secondary signal.

## Non-negotiables

- Every claim traces to a measured number.
- Careers are described with empirical occupational data, not imagination.
- Uncertainty is always shown.
- Never claim to measure neurochemistry or the brain, and never claim to predict success. "How you're wired" is a metaphor and may be used as one.
- The written prose is produced by Claude from structured results only. It never adds facts.
